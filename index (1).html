Common questions

How long does registration take?

It depends on the country and company type. We give you a realistic estimate on the intro call, before you commit to anything.

Do I need to be in the country to register?

Often not, but rules vary. We check the requirements for your situation and tell you what can be done remotely.

What documents will you ask for?

Usually identification, proof of address, and details of the company name, activity, and owners. You get an exact list up front.

Is the intro call really free?

Yes. It is a short conversation to see whether we are a good fit, with no obligation.

Tell us what you want to start.

Send a few lines about your business idea and where you plan to operate. We reply within two working days.
